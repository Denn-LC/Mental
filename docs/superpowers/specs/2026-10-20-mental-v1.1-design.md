# mental v1.1 — design

Date: 2026-10-20 · Branch: `v1.1` · Builds on: `2026-10-20-mental-v1-design.md` (everything there still
holds unless this file changes it).

## What changes and why

Feedback from playing v1.0:

1. Hints were bad, and a hint for mental arithmetic mostly gives the answer away anyway.
   → **Hints are removed** (from the screen and from the engine). Free Play gets **Give up**, which
   shows the answer and the worked steps.
2. The app dropped you straight into a game, it was keys-only, and 1 minute was too short.
   → A **home screen** and a Free Play **options** screen, both driven by arrow keys or the mouse.
   **Enter** and **Give up** buttons in Free Play. A **3-2-1 countdown** before every round.
   The test lasts **2 minutes**, has no options, and mixes all topics and all levels.
3. The look is still rough. → **Not in v1.1.** No visual design work until the user asks for it.
   Two exceptions requested now: **all text uses the pixel font**, and wrong answers get
   **feedback lines that depend on how close you were**.
4. Pointless questions. → **0 and 1 never appear** as a number in a question or a step.
   **Round numbers** (+10, +30, −500) are fine but **only at level 1**.
5. Fix the bugs deferred from v1.0.

## Layout

- The `mental` title sits at the top centre of every screen.
- The home and options screens sit in the middle of the window.
- In a game, the answer box sits in the middle of the window, the question just above it, and the
  3-2-1 countdown shows in that same spot.
- The answer box takes an optional leading minus and at most 5 digits (no answer is longer).

## Home screen

- Shown on load and after Esc from a game.
- Two big choices: **Free Play** and **Test Yourself · 2 min**. The highlighted one has an amber fill.
- Arrow keys move the highlight **the way they point** (to the nearest item that lies more in that
  direction than off to the side; nothing happens at an edge). The highlight also follows the mouse.
  Enter or a click goes in. Free Play is highlighted on load; after a game, the mode you just played.

## Free Play options

- **Topics**: one checkbox per category (all ticked by default). This is the topic picker the v1
  spec deferred; the API already takes a comma-separated category list.
- **Level**: 1 easy / 2 medium / 3 hard.
- **Play** button, highlighted on arrival.
- Arrow keys move the way they point, as on the home screen (topics form a grid, levels a row, Play
  below). Enter or Space ticks a topic or picks a level; Enter on Play plays. Mouse works too.
  Esc goes back home.
- No topic ticked → "Tick at least one topic." and stay here.

## Countdown

- Play (Free Play) or choosing Test Yourself shows **3, 2, 1** as a big number in the middle of the
  screen (0.7 s each), then the first question. The **answer box and buttons are hidden** during the
  countdown, and whenever there is no question to answer (e.g. after the test's time is up). Questions load during the countdown. Keys do nothing during it; Esc goes home.
- "Enter to go again" after a test also counts down.

## Free Play

- Enter (key or button) submits; when solved or given up, Enter (button reads "Next") goes on.
- Wrong → a feedback line (see below), stay on the question.
- **Give up** (button or `G`) → "the answer is N", the steps, button reads "Next".
  Recorded as `gave_up: true, correct: false`; the streak resets.
- Correct → "correct!" plus the steps. Streak counts answers right on the first try.
- No skip in Free Play (Give up replaces it). Esc → home.

## Test Yourself

- No options: every topic, and **each question gets a random level 1–3**, so easy and hard questions
  are mixed (no more "always ÷ 2" relationships or "always 10%" percentages).
- 2 minutes, timer shows `m:ss`, starting after the countdown.
- No buttons. Typing the right answer advances. **A wrong answer shows nothing** (feedback would be a
  hint); Enter on a wrong value is still recorded in `wrong_tries`. Tab skips; Esc → home.
- Results as in v1, then Enter to go again (after a 1-second guard) or Esc for home.

## Wrong-answer lines (Free Play only)

Tier by how far off: `diff = |given − answer|`, `gap = diff / max(|answer|, 10)`.

| Tier | When | Lines (random, never the same twice in a row) |
|---|---|---|
| close | `diff ≤ 1` or `gap ≤ 5%` | almost! · so close · nearly there · off by a hair |
| off | otherwise | not quite · nope, try again · not it · try again |
| way off | wrong sign or `gap > 50%` | are you even trying? · jesus. · that's not even close · ...what? · bold guess |

## Engine changes

- Strategies return `(text, answer, steps)`. `Question` loses `hints`.
- `make_questions(categories, difficulties, count)` takes a list of levels and picks a random
  (category, level) pair per question. `GET /api/questions?difficulty=` accepts `1`, `2`, `3` or `mixed`.
- Display uses a plain `-` for minus: the pixel font has no `−` glyph. The answer box still accepts both.
- **No 0 or 1** as a number in question text or on the left of any step. One exception:
  `near_round_add`/`near_round_subtract`, where "+ 200 − 1" *is* the trick.
- Basic add/subtract: the second number never ends in 1, and is round only at level 1.
- `basic_multiply` level 1 steps are just the times-table fact; level 2 never splits off a 1.
- `basic_divide` level 2 quotient never ends in 0 or 1. `balance_sum` never moves by 1.
- `signed_add` never cancels to 0.

## Attempt record

`hints_used` is replaced by `gave_up: bool`. Summary `accuracy` = correct on the first try;
`avg_time_ms` is over solved questions only; the summary adds a `gave_up` count.

## Bugs fixed

- Holding Tab (or retyping) while the question queue refills recorded the same question twice.
- Fast setting changes could let an older fetch win (now: every game/menu change bumps a session
  number and stale fetches are dropped).
- No message when results failed to load.
- No way to retry after questions failed to load (now Enter retries).
- The browser's blue text selection clashed with the theme.

## Not in v1.1

Visual redesign, persistence/personal bests, more benchmarks, adaptive practice, cheat detection,
quant mode, deployment.
